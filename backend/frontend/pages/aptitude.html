Quantitative • Time & Work Campus Entrance Round

A can complete a piece of work in 12 days and B in 16 days. If both work together for 4 days, what fraction of the total work remains unfinished?

A) 1/3
B) 5/12
C) 5/12 (7/12 completed, 5/12 remains)
D) 1/4
Step-by-Step Mathematical Solution
1. Work done by A in 1 day = 1/12
2. Work done by B in 1 day = 1/16
3. Combined work in 1 day = (1/12) + (1/16) = (4 + 3) / 48 = 7/48
4. Work done in 4 days = 4 × (7/48) = 7/12
5. Remaining unfinished work = 1 - (7/12) = 5/12
Logical • Blood Relations Standard Placement Exam

Pointing to a photograph, a woman says, "His mother is the only daughter of my mother." How is the woman related to the man in the photograph?

A) Mother
B) Sister
C) Daughter
D) Grandmother
Step-by-Step Deduction

"The only daughter of my mother" refers to the speaker herself (the woman). Therefore, the man's mother is the woman speaking. Hence, the woman is the Mother of the man in the photograph.
